Notes from the upload

    These are the pieces of information people can put in a report.

    1. Choose the fields people can use

      Done

      Tick each column that reports may show. Unticked columns stay hidden from everyone except administrators.

    2. Default comparison

      To let people compare by another text column (for example Industry), tick it under Choose the fields people can use.

      People can still switch to another approved column in the report builder. Saved reports keep what they compare.

    3. Names and display Optional

      Give the dataset and its fields friendlier names. Source values never change, and each report can still override these.

    4. Publish

      Published

    Advanced: indicator definitions

    Only needed for known indicator keys that require a particular unit or a computed ratio.
